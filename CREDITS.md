# Credits

Hollowmere Dispatch, version 0.16.

## Rain

Gentle rain loop by [Ylmir](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable), recorded at a window and released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The bed uses the second of the four loops.

## Music

"Comfortable Mystery" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com). Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Footsteps

Wood, muffled rug, and stone steps are derived from recordings by [swuing](https://freesound.org/people/swuing/) (footstep-wood, footstep-concrete), mastered in the [Footsteps on different surfaces](https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-on-different-surfaces) pack by congusbongus. Licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The rug step is the wood recording, low-passed so it sits softer indoors.

## Effects

Door, bell, pickup, use, click, ghost chime, and the soft murmur under dialogue are from [100 CC0 SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx) by rubberduck, released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The dog bark is "Dog barking mono" by Brandon Morris on OpenGameArt, used under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (the file is also offered as OGA-BY 3.0).

Volume and mute still follow the settings sliders. Rain and music loop, and both duck while a line of dialogue is on screen.

## Type

Cinzel and Cormorant Garamond, loaded through Fontsource. Both are under the SIL Open Font License.

## Pictures

The rooms, June, Ambrose, Edith, the dog, and the detectives are original art made for this game. Sara Lind and Skuggi are placeholder names in `src/detectives.ts`. Sara is drawn from a description only, not from a real person.
