# Credits

Hollowmere Dispatch, version 0.11.

## Audio

Every loop and effect is synthesised in the browser with the Web Audio API: rain, shop creaks, the stockroom drone, footsteps, the bell, doors, the lantern, and the rest. No third-party recordings are used.

## Type

Cinzel and Cormorant Garamond, loaded through Fontsource. Both are under the SIL Open Font License.

## Pictures

The rooms, June, Ambrose, Edith, and the detective are original art made for this game.
